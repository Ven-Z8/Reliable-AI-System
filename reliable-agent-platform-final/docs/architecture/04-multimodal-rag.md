# Multimodal RAG

## Purpose

Answer questions over long, heterogeneous PDFs with inspectable evidence.

## Structural retrieval

- section hierarchy;
- paragraphs;
- tables and cells;
- captions;
- bounding boxes;
- OCR confidence;
- parent-child relationships.

## Visual retrieval

- rendered pages;
- page embeddings;
- region crops;
- chart and diagram evidence;
- cross-modal reranking.

## Answer contract

- answer;
- answer type;
- confidence;
- document/page/block citations;
- bounding boxes;
- calculation formula and inputs;
- verifier result;
- abstention flag.
